## Mission

I design and build AI systems for organizations that need reliability at scale — not demos. My work centers on enterprise AI infrastructure, retrieval-augmented systems, autonomous agents, and the automation layer that connects AI to real business workflows. I approach every system as a product: engineered for maintainability, designed for adoption, and built to survive contact with production.
